# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Focus criticism on claims, evidence, and contributions rather than people.
- Disclose relevant commercial relationships.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, trolling, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery directed at another participant.
- Deliberate misinformation, fabricated evidence, spam, or undisclosed promotion.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Reports may be sent privately to the repository owner through the contact methods on their GitHub profile. Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of reporters.
