# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep Awesome Profitable AI accurate, useful, and independent. Product founders and employees are welcome to contribute, but every submission is reviewed under the same evidence standard.

Please read our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) before participating.

## Before You Submit

A product is eligible when it:

- Uses AI as a core part of the product, not only as a minor add-on.
- Is publicly accessible and actively maintained.
- Has a clear commercial signal supported by a direct public source.
- Fits an existing category or makes a compelling case for a new one.
- Is useful enough to merit curation rather than merely existing.

Do not submit:

- Pre-launch waitlists without commercial evidence.
- Unsourced revenue estimates or numbers copied from database aggregators.
- Generic agencies, courses, prompt collections, or affiliate roundups.
- Products whose main purpose is illegal, deceptive, or abusive activity.
- Referral links, tracking links, URL shorteners, or promotional copy.
- Duplicate products or multiple products that are only minor variants of one service.

## Evidence Labels

Use the strongest label that the linked source actually supports:

- **Revenue** - Revenue, ARR, MRR, GMV, or profitable status disclosed by the company, a founder, or reputable reporting.
- **Customers** - Paying customers or paid subscribers. A total user, download, or signup count does not qualify.
- **Funding** - A disclosed institutional funding round or acquisition. Funding is not revenue.
- **Paid product** - A working official pricing page when no stronger public evidence is available. A price does not prove profitability.

Prefer sources in this order:

1. Official company reports, announcements, or case studies.
2. Direct founder interviews or posts.
3. Reputable reporting that names its source.
4. The official pricing page, only for the `Paid product` label.

The evidence link should open the page containing the claim, not a search result, homepage, or secondary roundup. Include the source year when it is visible.

## Entry Format

Add one product to one category, in alphabetical order:

```markdown
- [Product](https://product.example) - Objective description. **Revenue:** Evidence claim ([source, 2026](https://evidence.example)).
```

Descriptions should start with an uppercase letter, use neutral language, and end with a period. Avoid claims such as “best,” “leading,” “revolutionary,” or “fastest.” Do not add logos, screenshots, discount codes, or more than one product link.

## Disclose Your Relationship

State whether you are a founder, employee, investor, affiliate, agency representative, customer, or unaffiliated community member. Self-submissions are allowed. Undisclosed conflicts of interest may result in removal.

## Submission Options

- For a new product, use the [product nomination form](https://github.com/lubanproj/awesome-profitable-ai/issues/new?template=add-product.yml).
- For a broken link, outdated claim, or correction, use the [problem report](https://github.com/lubanproj/awesome-profitable-ai/issues/new?template=report-problem.yml).
- For a ready-to-merge entry, open a pull request and complete the checklist.
